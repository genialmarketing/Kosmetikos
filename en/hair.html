Waxing & Hair Removal

Body

Body hair serves several important functions. It helps regulate body temperature, protects the skin and reduces friction between skin folds. Even so, many people today choose to remove unwanted hair growth, and at KosmetiKos we offer gentle hair removal with warm wax for both body and face, including the upper lip, chin, underarms, arms and legs.

Why choose waxing?

Waxing gives a long-lasting result since the hair is removed from the root, and you can enjoy smooth, hair-free skin for 4 to 6 weeks after treatment. With regular waxing, the new hairs that grow back often become finer and softer over time.

We use warm wax, which is gentle on the skin and helps minimise irritation, including for sensitive skin.

How to prepare for your appointment

For the best result, hair should be around 5mm long before your appointment. It also helps to gently exfoliate the skin the day before treatment, so the skin is ready for waxing.

For the first 24 hours after treatment, avoid sun exposure and protect the treated area from UV rays. Also avoid perfumed products and creams, as well as intense exercise, to prevent unnecessary sweating and irritation.

Frequently asked questions

What types of hair do we have on the body?
The body has two types of hair. Vellus hair is the short, light fuzz that covers most of the body. Terminal hair is the longer, darker and coarser hair found on the scalp, underarms and intimate areas, among other places.

What's the difference between waxing and other methods?
Shaving and hair removal creams cut or dissolve the hair at the surface, so it grows back quickly. Waxing, on the other hand, removes the entire hair follicle, giving a longer-lasting result. Laser and IPL are light treatments that reduce hair growth permanently over time.
